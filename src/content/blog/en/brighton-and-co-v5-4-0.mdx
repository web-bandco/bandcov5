# Brighton and Co V5 - Version 5.4.0

There are a number of key changes in v5.4.0:

- **Brand New About Page** - As part of the regeneration of the B&Co site, there is a new About Page.
- **Policy Hub** - A new Policy Hub containing the moved Terms and Conditions, a new Privacy Policy and a new AI Policy.
- **Visual Upgrades** - Sweeping new visual upgrades, enhancements and improvements have been made to the site, including substantial debloating that has significantly improved the site's performance.

There are also a few more minor changes and improvements:

- Backend updates
- General refinements.
